**Phnom Penh,**  – Poscar Digital Co., Ltd. proudly announces that its flagship accounting system, **Vithean** ([www.vithean.com](http://www.vithean.com)), has successfully integrated with the official **Cambodia E-Invoicing System** (www.e-invoice.gov.kh) under the Ministry of Economy and Finance. This milestone marks a major step forward in supporting small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) across Cambodia to digitize financial operations with greater ease, compliance, and transparency.

With this integration, Vithean becomes one of the first Cambodian-developed accounting platforms fully connected to the national e-invoicing infrastructure. Businesses using Vithean can now issue, manage, and transmit invoices electronically in full compliance with the Ministry of Economy and Finance’s digital invoicing requirements.

**Benefits of Cambodia E-Invoicing System for Businesses**

- **Timesaving on issuing invoices** – Faster, smarter billing processes.
- **Increased productivity** – Shift staff workload from manual admin to core business activities.
- **Enhanced transparency & accountability** – Build stronger trust in financial operations.
- **Strengthened digital governance** – Contributing to national leadership and attracting investments.
- **Environmental sustainability** – Reduce paper use and support green business practices.

**Vithean’s Advantage for SMEs**

- **Scalable for All Businesses** – Whether small retailers or mid-sized enterprises, Vithean provides the tools to grow with confidence in the digital economy.

**Benefits for the Financial Sector**

This integration not only simplifies compliance for SMEs but also contributes to building a stronger financial ecosystem in Cambodia. By adopting e-invoicing through Vithean, businesses help drive efficiency, reduce risks of error or fraud, and align with international best practices in digital finance.

**Mr. Sok Pongsametrey, COO of Poscar Digital (Vithean),** stated:

“At Vithean, our mission is to empower Cambodian SMEs with modern digital tools. By integrating with the Cambodia E-Invoicing System, we are enabling businesses to stay compliant while improving efficiency and transparency. This is more than a system upgrade—it is a step toward preparing Cambodian enterprises for the future of digital finance.”

**About Vithean**

**Vithean** is a cloud-based accounting platform designed for SMEs in Cambodia and the region. Offering features such as invoicing, financial reporting and integration with national digital finance systems, Vithean is built to simplify business management and accelerate digital transformation for enterprises of all sizes.
