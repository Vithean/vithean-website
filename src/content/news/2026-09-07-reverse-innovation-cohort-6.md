**Phnom Penh, September 2026** — **Techo Startup Center** has announced the nine startups advancing to the Acceleration Phase of **Reverse Innovation Cohort 6**, and Vithean is among them.

The selection followed **Elimination Pitch Day**, where teams presented to a panel of judges, domain experts and the programme committee.

## What the programme is

Reverse Innovation is a **three-month accelerator** that supports startups in developing innovative solutions to real market challenges, with guidance and mentorship from domain experts across different sectors.

It is organised by the **Ministry of Economy and Finance** and **Techo Startup Center**, and co-organised by the **Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology & Innovation** and **Khmer Enterprise**.

The nine teams advancing in Cohort 6 are XOFFICE, GOLOCA, TOBSAR, AAP FARM, Vithean (POSCAR Digital Co., Ltd.), SV.CY (Cambodia) IM-EX Co., Ltd, KAPPY, SADA Solution Co., Ltd and Dojology Media Network.

## Why it matters to us

Vithean is built in Phnom Penh by POSCAR Digital Co., Ltd., with practising accountants shaping what the system has to do. That is a deliberate way of working, and it is the part a structured accelerator tests hardest: not whether the software runs, but whether the problem it solves is the one the market actually has.

Three months of mentorship from people who assess that for a living is worth having. Our thanks to Techo Startup Center, the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the co-organisers for the place — and congratulations to the other eight teams.
